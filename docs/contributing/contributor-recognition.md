## 🎖️ Contributor Levels
1. **Novice:** First PR merged.
2. **Regular:** 5+ skills/features contributed.
3. **Core:** Significant architectural contributions and test coverage.
4. **Maintainer:** Voting rights on repository governance.

## 🎁 Benefits
- **Badges:** Earn verifiable digital badges for your GitHub README.
- **Swag:** Official bank-agent-skills branded stickers and t-shirts for top maintainers.
- **Speaking:** Opportunities to co-author case studies and present at architecture meetups.
