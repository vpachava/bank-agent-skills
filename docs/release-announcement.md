🚀 **Excited to announce the v1.0.1 release of `bank-agent-skills`!** 

Architecting complex systems in heavily regulated industries (Banking, Healthcare, Energy, Government) usually means getting bogged down in writing repetitive HLDs, compliance checklists, and static diagrams that drift from reality. 

We built **bank-agent-skills** to change that. It's a production-grade, agentic Solution Architecture Governance Engine that turns enterprise design into **Executable Code**.

### What's Inside v1.0.1:
- 🌐 **100 Active Skills** across 16 architectural domains (Discovery, Architecture, Security, Cloud, Data, AI/ML, and more).
- 🌍 **Multi-Jurisdictional Overlays:** Built-in compliance mapping for UAE (CBUAE/PDPL), KSA (SAMA), EU (DORA/GDPR), USA (OCC/NYDFS), UK, Singapore, Australia, and India.
- 🏥 **Industry Compliance Packs:** Specialized guardrails for Healthcare (HIPAA), Insurance (Solvency II), Retail (PCI-DSS v4.0), Government (NIST 800-53), and Energy (NERC CIP).
- 🔍 **Evidence-Based Verification:** Enforces that AI-generated designs link directly to real repository IaC/Config files—no more hallucinations.
- 📊 **Diagram-as-Code (`/archify`):** Instantly compiles design models into canonical Mermaid.js specifications.
