# /industries/energy

## Overview
Architectural framework for critical infrastructure, power grids, smart metering, and SCADA control systems. Enforces NERC CIP cybersecurity requirements and IEC 62443 industrial control system network zoning standards.

## Process
1. **Critical Asset Classification (NERC CIP):** Identify and classify Bes Cyber Assets (BCAs) into Low, Medium, or High impact.
2. **Industrial Network Zoning (IEC 62443):** Isolate corporate IT networks from Operational Technology (OT) control networks using strict DMZ zones and conduits.
3. **Smart Grid Security:** Secure edge advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) with device cryptographic identities.
