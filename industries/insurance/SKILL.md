# /industries/insurance

## Overview
Architectural framework for insurance, reinsurance, and insurtech systems. Enforces Solvency II Pillar 1-3 risk reporting, IFRS 17 insurance contract accounting engine architecture, and NAIC cybersecurity standards.

## Process
1. **Actuarial Cash Flow Engine:** Design high-throughput cash-flow calculation pipelines for IFRS 17 contract groups.
2. **Solvency II Risk Integration:** Connect policy administration systems to Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR) engines.
3. **Claims Automation:** Model automated claims processing with anti-fraud scoring gates.
